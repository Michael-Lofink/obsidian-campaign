**Source** Custom campaign NPC; thaumaturge treasure thief
**Perception** +11; low-light vision

**Regalia** While holding his orb, Big gains a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, and Intimidation; these bonuses are not included above. Allies who see him can Follow the Expert for his trained skills, gaining a +1 circumstance bonus when he is only trained.

**HP** 48
**Inspiring Regalia** (aura, emotion, mental, visual) 15 feet. While Big holds his orb, he and allies in the aura who can see him gain a +1 status bonus to saves against fear. When he normally reduces his frightened value at the end of his turn, he also reduces each ally's frightened value in the aura by 1.

**Speed** 25 feet

**Melee** `[one-action]` thirsting dagger +12 [+8/+4] (agile, finesse, magical, versatile S), **Damage** 2d4+4 piercing

This replaces his previous exploitation.
**Critical Success** Learn all its weaknesses, resistances, and immunities, including amounts; choose mortal weakness or personal antithesis.

**Failure** Use personal antithesis only.
**Critical Failure** No exploitation; Big is off-guard until his next turn starts.
**Mortal Weakness** His Strikes trigger the highest discovered weakness against the target and creatures of that exact type, without changing damage type.
**Personal Antithesis** The target alone gains weakness 4 to Big's weapon and unarmed Strikes.
On any result except critical failure, his Strikes against the target become magical. Exploitation lasts until he uses this action again. Apply the chosen weakness separately from listed damage; personal antithesis and mortal weakness do not stack.
