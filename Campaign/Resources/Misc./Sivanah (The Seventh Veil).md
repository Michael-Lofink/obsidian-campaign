Sivanah is the mirror world. The mirror world is Sivanah. Much like a living organism, Sivanah is made up of smaller organisms. The entire realm of mirrors is one being with consciousness: Sivanah. Sivanah is not a god, just a Primordial or Infinite Manifestation.

[[The Mirror]] known as The Seventh Witness is a direct fragment of Sivanah's realm rather than an imitation. It made its way into the Material Plane / The Universe from the Maelstrom where some part houses the physical space of Sivanah's realm.

Sivanah, much like an eldritch being, exists at a scope beyond that of any denizen of any plane.
