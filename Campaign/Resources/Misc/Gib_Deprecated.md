## Provided Details
### Backstory
Big was a 25 year old Thaumaturgist Tripkee who specialized in collecting old relics from abandoned dungeons to turn them into implements. His childhood was mostly filled with reading about relics, or seeing what he could get away with in the local towns whilst managing mostly on his own as a bit of a hermit. He lived alone the majority of his life, getting lost one day as a child and never finding home. He was a little scrawny yet conniving, not used to getting in many fights - more focused on the money from selling relics. The money was originally gathered to simply sustain himself, but after getting a better eye for what relics will sell, he became more and more greedy. After a particularly good haul from some nearby rundown temple he didn't bother researching, he managed to get an item that had been requested by a buyer, named Molak, on the market for a while. It was a mirror he could sense had some underlying magic to it, and the designs of the mirror certainly indicated it was special. It had a lightly carved face on the back, all whilst glittering with a gold rim. He found it in a chest that had been hidden from sight, with what looked like it could've been centuries of dirt and grime covering it. The seller he had lined up didn't seem like an upstanding citizen, but he didn't particularly care as long as he was paid. The gentleman was quite large and imposing as a human dromaar, with a group of lackeys with him at all times. The night of the deal was upon them, but Big was used to this type of trade off. While he was normally anxious and squirrelly, he presented calm and confident as he pulled out the mirror relic, not wanting his buyer to think he had more leverage than he did. Big eyed the stacks of gold pieces the lackeys were counting one last time. Big could feel the mirror he was holding beginning to simmer with magical power, but he brushed it off, thinking it was just something the relic did and was more focused on the shiny gold. After both parties were ready, Big began to hand off the mirror. Both the buyer and Big were holding part of the mirror as it began to shake. They looked at it confused as the reflection shown in the mirror of Big began to wobble and shift. After another moment, the reflection began to move seemingly on its own in the mirror, before pressing its hand against the glass. Before Big or the buyer could react, the hand pushed through and started coming out of the mirror. A nearly identical looking frog to Big tumbled out of the mirror and onto the ground. Every person in the run down shack they were using for a trade off stared in confusion at the frog on the ground. The frog blinked a few times and gained his bearings before looking back to the mirror he just came out of. Feeling a connection to the mirror, and knowing it was about to be sold, he snatched the mirror in the confusion and jumped out a window. The buyer and Big stared back at each other incredulously, before both looking to the gold pile. Neither was certain if the transfer was complete, only that some frog who looked like the Thaumaturgist just stole the rare relic.

That frog ran for what felt like days until he reached a town. He was haggard and conflicted. He had no real idea who he was, or if he was even really his own person. He remembered the life of Big, but it clearly felt like someone else's memories he was just copying. All he knew was that his existence was attached to this cursed mirror. He managed to find an inn, and was surprised that the money he found in his pocket was accepted. After much deliberation that night, he decided to live as Gib and to stay as far away from Big and that buyer as possible.
The next two years Gib spent focused on building an emergency fund. He managed to get a very small home in a worse part of town, but made sure to have 4-5 locks on his residence at any point in time. The origin of his life made him extremely paranoid that Big or the buyer would return, wanting to claim the mirror that founded his existence. Because of this, he always kept a go bag ready, and his small measly emergency fund to get out of the town as quickly as possible. Despite that, he did have a nice job in town as a laborer, with coworkers who made him think hard about himself. Was he a new person? Or was he just that Thaumaturgist in a new body? Could he make it his own life regardless? Questions constantly raked his mind, as he craved to figure out who he was and his identity. He wanted to make friends, but tended to keep people at a distance, for fear they would rat him out. Most of his life is driven by fear and intrigue.

### Superficial Personality
Most people from afar would see Gib as a paranoid and quiet creature. They wouldn’t sense danger or malice from him ever despite the subtle worry that his skin is poisonous, and would end up being more confused by his actions than anything. He would seem to always be in his own world, and looking over his shoulder like something might happen. Someone asked about his mirror he always carried once, but after he looked terrified and ran off, no one seemed to bother mentioning it again. People tend to stay away, but won’t cross the street to avoid walking past him. This has seemed to become more common the longer Gib stays in this town.

His coworkers would see him in a bit kinder light, almost like dealing with an injured animal. They understand his actions are seemingly coming from a good place, and tend to ignore the small little strange things he may do. They are a bit concerned about him, especially since he seems to get very defensive about his life prior to moving to the town. No one has been over to his home, nor has anyone invited him into theirs, but they have all gone out to eat lunch together. Ultimately, they view him as a bit strange but mostly with kindness behind it.

## Features
### Low-Light Vision
You can see in [dim light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=408) as though it were [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=407), so you ignore the [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=62) condition due to dim light.
### Natural Climber
You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [Athletics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=36) checks to [Climb](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2374).
### Snaptongue Tripkee
Your tongue is especially long, and you can launch it with extraordinary range and precision. You can use your tongue to deliver spells with a range of touch and perform extremely simple Interact actions, such as opening some types of unlocked doors. Your tongue can't perform actions that require fingers or significant manual dexterity, including any action that would require a check to accomplish, and you can't use it to hold items.
### Reflection
You were created as a duplicate of another creature, intentionally or accidentally, though you might not know of your origins. Other than a minor mark or two, you look just like your progenitor. You gain the [reflection](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=902) trait, in addition to the traits from your ancestry. You don't need to attempt Deception checks to [Impersonate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2388) your progenitor unless you're interacting with people who know them personally or you do something known to be out of character for them. The GM might require you to roll a Deception check in other circumstances, such as if you're mirror-risen and interacting with someone who has seen an accurate likeness of your progenitor and might notice a distinguishing feature on the reverse side. You can choose from reflection feats and feats from your ancestry whenever you gain an ancestry feat.
