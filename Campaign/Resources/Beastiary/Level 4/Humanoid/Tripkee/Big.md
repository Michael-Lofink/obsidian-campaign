## Playing Big

This is a custom level 4 NPC built with creature statistics and selected thaumaturge abilities. It is a compact sheet for Maggie to play in the dream, rather than a complete PC build with every ancestry, background, and class choice. His skills and saves use NPC benchmarks. Equipment, languages, and the choice of a regalia implement are proposed additions to the campaign; the orb matches the object in his miniature.

Big is observant, practiced at lying, and good at spotting something he can sell. His Treasure Lore represents practical trade experience. He can recognize profitable salvage while knowing very little about the people who made it. When holding his orb, use **Deception +13, Diplomacy +11, and Intimidation +11**. Putting the orb away frees a hand for stealing, picking locks, or carrying loot, but ends its held benefits.

For a physical obstacle, use **Acrobatics +10** to Escape or squeeze, **Athletics +8** to Climb, and **Thievery +12** for locks, traps, or concealed valuables. Ordinary skill actions still require their normal tools, actions, and circumstances. Pickpocket does not make theft undetectable. Big's Thievery is expert for proficiency-gated uses; his other listed skills are trained, except Esoteric Lore, Deception, and Stealth, which are expert.

If a fight starts, his usual opening is to hold the orb, Exploit Vulnerability, and Strike. Personal antithesis adds 4 damage when a Strike damages its target. That weakness damage is applied after calculating the Strike and is not doubled on a critical hit. His agile attacks have MAP –4/–8. Throwing his only dagger leaves him without it until he retrieves it.

## Big in Enzi's Hovel

**Suggested starting state after Enzi's treatment:** 12/48 HP, conscious and stable. Use his usual skills and saves. The bruising and swollen eye can remain descriptive; additional conditions would make this short social scene harder to run. This is a scene setup, not the Weak creature adjustment, and his level remains 4.

For a playable default, Enzi has set Big's recovered belongings beside the bed and kept his dagger on the workbench while treating him. Big's orb and esoterica are among those belongings. These are proposed staging details: decide which possessions survived Molak's men before play. If Big has removed his studded leather for treatment, use **AC 18** until he puts it back on. If his dagger is missing, a mundane knife uses **+11 [+7/+3]**, dealing **1d4+2** piercing with empowerment, or **1d4** without it.

The orb is Big's bonded implement; the mirror Gib took is a separate object. Big can use his regalia abilities without recovering that mirror. If the orb is out of reach, he cannot use Exploit Vulnerability or Implement's Empowerment, and loses the regalia bonuses and aura. He can still use his skills, including Esoteric Lore. His orb requires one hand, so handling a knife and harvesting or packing leechwort may require putting it down.

Use **Gib's Will modifier** for the DC 25 attempt to override Big's intended theft. Big's +14 Will does not apply to that story check. Root to Life remains Gib's proposed reward; it is not included in Big's abilities. The leechwort knowledge can pass to Gib whether the theft happens or is prevented, provided Big learned it from Enzi.
