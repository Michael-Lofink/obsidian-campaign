The freezing ooze clings to those struck and hardens into thick sheets of ice. At the start of its turn, a creature still immobilized by the ice takes 4d6 cold damage. Another creature can free a frozen target by dealing a total of 20 fire damage to the frozen target. Left unattended, the ice crumbles away in 1 minute on its own. Creatures with the [fire](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=604) trait can't be frozen in place by Icemire Breath. Flying creatures [fall](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=402) if frozen, and swimming creatures that are frozen rise toward the surface of the water at a speed of 60 feet per round. The linnorm can't use Icemire Breath again for 1d4 rounds.
