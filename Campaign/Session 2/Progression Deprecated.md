# Recap
Last time, Gib began an ordinary day in Neeb. He went to work, helped reinforce the town’s walls, and received a broken pocket watch from Bill.

After his shift, Terrell asked Gib to deliver a wrapped blade to the reclusive blacksmith Tilda. When Gib reached her home, he found two kholo outside and Tilda dead within. Hiding inside was a blood-covered child.

Unable to take the child through his mirror, Gib told him to wait, then run for Neeb. Gib drew the kholo away and escaped them through the forest.

When he reached an overlook, Gib found Neeb burning. Raiders had breached the walls. He watched Bill die in the attack, then saw his own home consumed by flames.

Standing before it was Molak.

Across the smoke and distance, Molak turned and looked directly at Gib.

That is where we begin.
