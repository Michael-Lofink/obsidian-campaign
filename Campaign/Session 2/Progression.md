# Session 2 — The Road to Eeler’s Inn

## Session Goals

Gib escapes Molak’s hunters, follows the Vanji River south, discovers signs of Sivanah’s influence, obtains practical equipment, and reaches Eeler’s Inn.

## Important Treasure

- Overgrown Shrine: Lesser Smoke Ball and Repair Toolkit
    
- Knight’s Refuge: Reedscale Vest, Thieves’ Toolkit, Minor Healing Potion, and local map
    
- Gib already possesses an Adventurer’s Pack
    

## Encounter Guardrails

Gib is alone and 1st level.

During the kholo encounter, only one kholo should engage Gib directly. The second kholo serves as an approaching threat that pressures him to escape.

At Knight’s Refuge, use either the Giant Centipede or the modified level –1 Centipede Swarm as the primary enemy. Do not place both in combat simultaneously.

# Scene 2: Escaping the Hunt

**Song:** _The Beast_ from _Sicario_ by Jóhann Jóhannsson

The two kholos are searching for Gib. They have been ordered to capture him alive.

Gib can hide, flee, create a diversion, prepare an ambush, or attempt another plan.

## Information Gib Can Overhear

Use these lines during pauses in the search:

> “He was seen on the ridge.”

> “Then he cannot have gone far.”

> “Remember the order. Alive.”

If Gib remains hidden long enough:

> “Molak will want an explanation if we return without him.”

This confirms that the attack and pursuit are connected to Molak.

---

## Hiding Procedure

Use the 6-by-6-square overlook map.

1. Roll initiative. Gib uses Stealth. The kholos use Perception.
    
2. Gib begins undetected if he hides before they enter.
    
3. Each round, the kholos move through the map.
    
4. Only one kholo uses Seek during each round.
    
5. The other kholo guards an exit, investigates signs of movement, or speaks.
    
6. Gib escapes by reaching the southern edge of the map or remaining undiscovered for five rounds.
    

The kholos’ Seek checks use Perception +7 or +8 against Gib’s Stealth DC.

Remember:

- A successful Seek can make Gib hidden instead of undetected.
    
- A critical success can reveal Gib immediately.
    
- Foliage, rocks, elevation, and darkness can provide concealment or cover.
    
- Creating a Diversion can redirect the searchers.
    
- Survival can be used to conceal tracks or create a false trail.
    

## Useful Improvised Actions

Allow reasonable skill checks for the following:

**Create a false trail:** Survival DC 15  
One kholo spends its next turn investigating the wrong direction.

**Throw an object or imitate a sound:** Deception DC 15  
Functions as Create a Diversion.

**Climb into the trees:** Athletics DC 14  
Success provides greater cover. Critical failure causes a noisy fall.

**Identify a hidden route down the ridge:** Survival DC 14  
Success reveals a faster path to the southern edge.

**Use the mirror:** Resolve according to Gib’s established mirror abilities and limitations.

## Search Complications

Use one complication each round if needed:

- Loose stones shift beneath Gib’s feet.
    
- A frightened bird launches from nearby foliage.
    
- Smoke blows across the ridge and briefly blocks sight.
    
- One kholo stops only a few feet from Gib’s hiding place.
    
- The kholos separate and search opposite sides of the map.

## If Gib Is Discovered

One kholo reaches Gib first. Use the **Guard** stat block.

The Bodyguard begins off-map and approaches once combat starts.

### Bodyguard Arrival Timer

At the end of round 1:

> A signal whistle cuts through the jungle.

At the end of round 2:

> Heavy footsteps crash through the brush behind the guard.

At the end of round 3, the Bodyguard reaches the edge of the map.

The Bodyguard’s arrival signals that Gib needs to escape. Gib can flee from the map without defeating the guard.

The kholos want Gib alive. They use nonlethal attacks whenever practical.

## Ending the Hunt

The hunt ends when Gib:

- Reaches the southern edge of the map.
    
- Breaks line of sight and successfully Hides.
    
- Disables the active pursuer and escapes before reinforcements arrive.
    
- Uses another reasonable plan to lose the kholos.

# Scene 3: Following the Vanji

Ask:

**“As Gib travels, does he prioritize speed, concealment, or searching for supplies?”**

## Speed

Use Athletics or Survival, DC 15.

**Success:** Gib reaches the shrine quickly.

**Failure:** Gib becomes fatigued from the rushed pace. The fatigue ends after ten minutes of rest.

## Concealment

Use Stealth or Survival, DC 15.

**Success:** Gib leaves no obvious trail.

**Failure:** Gib notices distant movement behind him. The kholos remain somewhere on his trail.

## Searching for Supplies

Use Survival or Perception, DC 15.

**Success:** Gib finds one useful natural resource, such as edible fruit, clean water, or medicinal herbs. Treat the herbs as granting a +1 circumstance bonus to one Treat Wounds check.

**Failure:** Gib loses time and finds signs that animals have recently fled the area.

# Scene 6: The Infestation

## Trigger

When Gib moves or touches the Reedscale Vest:

> The woven reeds buckle in your hands.
> 
> Dozens of pale legs begin moving beneath the surface.
> 
> Centipedes pour from the armor and scatter across the floor.
> 
> A much larger shape drops from inside the vest.
> 
> It strikes the ground, curls around itself, and raises a pair of dripping mandibles.

Roll initiative.

## Recommended Encounter

Use one **Giant Centipede, Creature –1**.

The smaller centipedes remain environmental description. They do not roll initiative or attack.

## Environmental Pressure

At the end of each round, describe the infestation spreading.

Round 1:

> More centipedes crawl from the vest and disappear between the floorboards.

Round 2:

> The insects begin gathering near the hovel’s doorway.

Round 3:

> The floor around the vest is alive with movement.

## Ways to End the Encounter

### Defeat the Giant Centipede

The remaining centipedes scatter once the largest specimen is killed.

### Use the Smoke Ball

The smoke drives the insects out of the hovel. The Giant Centipede spends its next turn moving away from the smoke.

If the hovel remains filled with smoke, the encounter ends once Gib leaves the building.

### Remove the Vest

Gib can carry, drag, or throw the vest outside.

The Giant Centipede follows the nest.

### Lure the Centipede Toward the Beetles

Gib can lead the centipede toward the beetle colony outside.

Once the centipede enters the beetles’ territory:

> The grass erupts with yellow-black bodies.
> 
> Beetles swarm over the centipede as it twists and strikes at them. Within seconds, both colonies disappear into the undergrowth in a mass of clicking shells and thrashing legs.

The encounter ends.

### Use Another Solution

Reward any reasonable use of fire, water, barriers, tools, or the environment.

## Alternate Swarm Encounter

Use the modified level –1 **Centipede Swarm** in place of the Giant Centipede if you want the infestation to feel larger.

Do not use both creatures at the same time.

The swarm can be dispersed by:

- Smoke
    
- Fire
    
- Flooding part of the hovel
    
- Removing the vest
    
- Luring it toward the beetles
    

## After the Encounter

The Reedscale Vest remains usable once the insects are removed.

> With the infestation gone, the refuge becomes quiet again.
> 
> Outside, the river continues south.
> 
> According to the map, Eeler’s Inn is only a few hours away.

Allow Gib to rest, Treat Wounds, repair equipment, or investigate the refuge further.
