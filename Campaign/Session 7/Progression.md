# Recap
The clock ticks after midnight, and so we pass through the looking glass, into Jaha

As Eeler's Inn cemented itself as a permanent fixture of the Western Annex, Gib started out with his new companion Squibbles to explore the city of Jaha. Near immediately, Gib was stopped by John Freeman the Port-Reeve of the Western Annex. Mr. Freeman pushed for answers about the appearance of the inn, and Gib conceded: bringing Freeman into the inn which exposed Eeler and his lichdom. Freeman began to flee, Gib started after him, but his efforts were futile, and with a now ticking clock he needed a plan. Speaking with Eeler, who had no idea how to uproot the inn once more nor much of a desire to do so, a plan to disguise him was concocted: Gib would need a Potion of Disguise.

Setting off into the Annex looking for a shop rather than to explore, Gib sped past Shora Mujafor, a woman interrogating a perhaps drunken fisherman about an inn that dropped from the sky, with no time to stop another potential actor from causing a headache down the line. Asking around, checking local signage, Gib came across *Hagatha's Cauldrons and Potions*, bartering with the storekeep while short 9 gold pieces, Gib agreed to leave collateral-- a lesser smoke ball and bedroll-- and come the next night to gather herbs for Hagatha's tinctures. Saddled with a Lesser Potion of Dwarven Disguise, Gib made his way back swiftly to the inn, and upon opening the main door found Shora and Eeler locked in combat.

At first, Shora demanded Gib leave the place unaware of his involvement with Eeler, but when Gib took a side, Shora's actions escalated. Over a matter of moments, Gib plead from his heart for the violence to end, for a moment to explain himself, and as he chose vulnerability and to share his story, Gib was able to calm Shora for a moment. Gib explained why he was in town both by happenstance and with a plan to find evidence of Molak, and Shora made a proposition: Gib does her a favor, she can do him a favor. Go to the old Silt House five minutes south as the crow flies and find a missing surveyor or at least the folio he'd been carrying. 

During this time Eeler disguised himself with the potion, and not a moment too soon as John Freeman returned with a band of guards demanded the lich to reveal himself. Shora, intervened, and pulled imaginary rank sending him off, but now both Shora and Gib were on a tight timeline, and they both would have to meet back by the break of day. 

And with that, we find ourselves with Gib. We settle at a short distant view of his face, a sweat bead trickling down from his brow leaving a thin misty trace as it comes to rest on his lip. Deep sockets are forming under his eyes, exhaustion. And, the small little rat atop his head, skitters down to his shoulder and begins chittering in Gib's ear in some seeming attempt to cheer him up.
