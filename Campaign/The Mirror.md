## Fauna of Mirrors
“In those days the world of mirrors and the world of men were not, as they are now, cut off from each other. They were, besides, quite different; neither beings nor colours nor shapes were the same. Both kingdoms, the specular and the human, lived in harmony; you could come and go through mirrors. One night the mirror people invaded the earth. Their power was great, but at the end of bloody warfare the magic arts of the Yellow Emperor prevailed. He repulsed the invaders, imprisoned them in their mirrors, and forced on them the task of repeating, as though in a kind of dream, all the actions of men. He stripped them of their power and of their forms and reduced them to mere slavish reflections. Nonetheless, a day will come when the magic spell will be shaken off.

The first to awaken will be the Fish. Deep in the mirror we will perceive a very faint line and the colour of this line will be like no other colour. Later on, other shapes will begin to stir. Little by little they will differ from us; little by little they will not imitate us. They will break through the barriers of glass or metal and this time will not be defeated. Side by side with these mirror creatures, the creatures of water will join the battle.”

# The Gilt Wound
Sivanah is the living mirror-world, a vast organism made of reflections, secrets, veils, and mirror-fauna. Long ago, something wounded her from the material side, and that wound was bound into a physical object: a gold-rimmed mirror with a carved face on the back. The mirror is not designed to create beings. It is a sealed breach, a living scar, and a direct access point into Sivanah’s body.

Molak is a cancerous rogue reflection, originally organized around the dominant ego of a human dromaar. He contains multiple assimilated mirror-consciousnesses inside himself. He wanted this specific mirror because it would let him access Sivanah’s living body and begin spreading into it.

When Big tried to sell the mirror, Molak touched the wound. Big was touching it too, so Big’s reflection was caught in the surface. Molak’s cancerous essence made contact with Sivanah’s living tissue, and the wound reacted violently. It attempted to seal, reject, and isolate the malignancy. In that reaction, living mirror-substance was expelled through Big’s reflected shape.

That expelled substance became Gib.

Gib has Big’s face and memories because Big walas the available reflection-tempte. Gib is linked to Molak because Molak’s corruption triggered and contaminated his creation. Gib has no reflection because he is made of reflective tissue. His back does not properly exist because he was born from a front-facing image and still has one metaphysical side oriented toward Sivanah.

Gib began as a defensive reaction.

Gib is becoming a person.
