# Overview

After witnessing the destruction of Neeb and seeing Molak outside his burning home, Gib escaped pursuing kholos and fled south along the Vanji River. Along the journey he discovered an ancient shrine dedicated to Sivanah, survived a dangerous encounter with giant centipedes, secured a temporary refuge, learned new information about his mirror implement, and eventually arrived at Eeler's Inn where he encountered an unknown occupant.

---

# Confirmed Events

## Escape from Neeb

Gib watched Neeb burn from an overlook and saw Molak standing outside the remains of his home.

While fleeing, he was pursued by kholos who attempted to track him through the jungle.

Gib successfully hid from them and escaped without being discovered.

After losing his pursuers, he continued south toward Eeler's Inn.

---

## Forced March

Wanting to put distance between himself and both Molak and the kholos, Gib traveled through the night.

As a result, he became Fatigued.

---

## Discovery of the Shrine

While traveling, Gib discovered an overgrown riverside shrine.

Features included:

- Seven carved veils surrounding a reflective basin.
    
- The central veil had been destroyed.
    
- Six remaining veils depicted:
    
    - Human
        
    - Elf
        
    - Halfling
        
    - Gnome
        
    - Anadi
        
    - Naga
        

Using Esoteric Lore, Gib identified the site as being connected to Sivanah, the Seventh Veil.

Within the shrine he found:

- A Lesser Smoke Ball.
    
- A Repair Toolkit.
    

While examining the reflective basin, Gib briefly noticed unusual movement within the reflection.

He chose not to investigate further and continued south.

---

## Arrival at Knight's Refuge

Gib reached an abandoned riverside camp called Knight's Refuge.

During his investigation he discovered:

- A missing boat.
    
- A rope that had been cut.
    
- A map showing local landmarks.
    

The map included:

- Neeb
    
- Knight's Refuge
    
- Eeler's Inn
    
- The Sivanah shrine
    
- A stone outcrop
    

Important details:

- Neeb had been crossed out.
    
- The ink appeared fresher than the rest of the map.
    
- Hidden writing revealed the phrase:
    
    - "One light means occupied."
        

Gib interpreted this as useful information regarding Eeler's Inn.

No explanation for the note was discovered.

---

## Centipede Encounter

Inside a hovel at Knight's Refuge, Gib investigated a reed vest hanging near a bunk.

This triggered an encounter with:

- A Giant Centipede.
    
- A Centipede Swarm.
    

During the fight:

- Gib suffered multiple injuries.
    
- Nearly became poisoned several times.
    
- Spent all three Hero Points.
    
- Discovered a Minor Healing Potion.
    

Using his reflection-based movement ability, Gib escaped across the river.

The encounter ended when a colony of beetles emerged and dragged the centipedes underground.

---

## Refuge Secured

After the fight, Gib returned to the refuge.

He discovered the reed vest had been infested with centipede eggs.

After removing the infestation, he claimed the armor.

Additional items recovered:

- Reedscale Vest
    
- Minor Healing Potion
    
- Thieves' Toolkit
    
- Various trinkets and odds-and-ends
    

Gib spent the remainder of the day:

- Treating his wounds.
    
- Repairing part of the refuge.
    
- Resting.
    

---

## Mirror Discovery

While using Exploit Vulnerability on a beetle, Gib experienced something unusual.

Instead of recalling information through study or memory, knowledge appeared to come directly from the creature.

As a result, Gib learned additional information about his mirror implement.

Confirmed traits discovered:

- Unique
    
- Artifact
    
- Divine
    
- Invested
    
- Magical
    
- Occult
    

He also learned of an ability called:

**Exploit Dominion**

The full nature of this ability remains unknown.

---

## Dream

While resting, Gib experienced a dream involving Molak.

In the dream:

- Molak appeared as a shifting collection of faces rather than a single individual.
    
- The dream focused on Gib's creation and Molak's hostility toward him.
    
- Molak expressed a desire to "return to her."
    

No explanation was provided regarding the meaning of the dream.

---

## Journey to Eeler's Inn

After resting, Gib resumed travel.

Following the map, he eventually reached Eeler's Inn.

The inn appeared largely abandoned.

One candle burned within.

Remembering the note on the map, Gib concluded that the inn was occupied.

Gib called out to the occupant and stated:

- The person had been at Knight's Refuge.
    
- They knew about Molak.
    

The unseen occupant responded:

> "You're wrong about a lot of things."

The occupant then asked whether Gib had arrived alone.

After Gib answered, locks began opening from inside the inn.

The session ended before the identity of the occupant was revealed.

# Gib's Beliefs and Assumptions

These are conclusions Gib reached during play, not confirmed facts.

- The person at Eeler's Inn may have previously been at Knight's Refuge.
    
- The person at Eeler's Inn may know about Molak.
    
- The map's owner may have crossed out Neeb after learning of its destruction.

Some of these may prove true, false, or only partially correct.
