Session 7 picked up with Gib already on his way south toward the Old Silt House, still exhausted from the events surrounding Eeler’s Inn. With only a few hours before he was expected to meet Shora again at daybreak, Gib chose to follow the western bank of the Olongo and move cautiously, searching for hazards as he went.

The Old Silt House proved to be an early piece of Western Annex infrastructure, built when the settlement first needed a rudimentary way to drain floodwater back into the Olongo. Newer infrastructure has since replaced it, leaving the building abandoned and badly deteriorated despite being only around a decade old. Someone had been there recently. A cloth warning perimeter had been torn open, muddy tracks led directly to the entrance, and water was actively moving through channels that should have belonged to a disused system.

Gib entered carefully and began examining the records left around the building. In one room, older plans and newer measurements appeared not to agree. Gib came away with the impression that the current measurements made the structure seem larger than the old schematics indicated, perhaps leaving some unaccounted-for space. This information came through Dubious Knowledge, however, so Gib cannot rely on that conclusion yet. Elsewhere, maintenance records and more recent notes also seemed inconsistent. Something recorded as maintained or properly serviced did not match the physical condition described in later paperwork, leaving Gib suspicious that at least one old maintenance report may have been false.

While moving through the deteriorated upper floor, Gib was ambushed by a crocodile from the water below. It dragged him into the channel and badly injured him, though Gib escaped through his mirror and retreated to higher ground. After an hour of treating his wounds, he returned to full health and continued searching while keeping his distance from the water.

Gib eventually spotted the missing surveyor’s body on a lower section near the river channel. The corpse had clearly been bitten by the crocodile, but a closer examination revealed another injury near the bite: a smaller, cleaner puncture that did not resemble the animal’s teeth. The surveyor’s belt had also been damaged or torn away. Gib correctly began treating the death as potentially more complicated than a crocodile attack.

The surveyor’s field folio was eventually found in another room of the Silt House. It had previously gotten wet and dried afterward, and a substantial section had been torn from it with what appeared to be a bite. The surviving notes recorded several concerning findings: sediment buildup was lighter than expected for the season, water was flowing through a channel previously recorded as clogged, tool or chisel marks were present where natural erosion should have been, and at least one warning marker had been displaced. The surveyor believed someone had been interfering with the Silt House and considered the situation worth further investigation.

The missing fragment of the folio was nowhere in the room. Gib remembered seeing something caught around the crocodile’s mouth earlier and decided the missing section might still be with the animal. He tracked the crocodile beneath one of the surviving sections of the structure and attempted to ambush it himself, briefly turning the encounter into an extremely questionable display of Australian crocodile-handling technique.

Gib wounded it, escaped, and then attempted the same maneuver again. The crocodile was waiting for him. Its readied jaws Strike critically hit Gib for 23 damage, leaving him at **1 HP**, though Gib successfully escaped the grab and teleported out of its reach.

Session 7 ended with Gib abandoning the fight for the moment, glaring back at the crocodile and promising:

> “I’ll be back.”

### Current State

**Gib:** 1/24 HP unless healing occurs immediately after the cut. He has the surviving field folio and knows the surveyor is dead. He has strong evidence that somebody interfered with the Silt House before the surveyor died.

**Crocodile:** Alive, injured, and still occupying the Silt House waterways. Gib suspects it may have the missing piece of the field folio.

**Surveyor:** Dead. Crocodile damage is present on the body, along with a smaller and more precise wound that Gib identified as inconsistent with the bite.

**Old Silt House:** Abandoned infrastructure that should have little reason to be operating, yet water is moving through at least one previously clogged route. Gib also turned the old control wheel one notch counterclockwise, increasing the flow through one channel.

**Field folio:** Recovered, water-damaged, and missing a torn section. Its surviving observations indicate deliberate interference with the drainage system.

### Things I would keep unresolved

The possible discrepancy between the building’s old schematics and its current measurements should stay uncertain because it came from Dubious Knowledge. Whether the folio was moved after the surveyor died is also currently **Gib’s inference**, based on finding it away from the body after it had gotten wet and dried. And while the puncture wound strongly raises the possibility of murder, Gib has not established the weapon, killer, motive, or even conclusively established that the puncture caused the surveyor’s death.

Those three uncertainties give you plenty to build the Western Annex conspiracy from without committing yourself yet to what is actually underneath the Silt House or exactly how the surveyor was killed.
