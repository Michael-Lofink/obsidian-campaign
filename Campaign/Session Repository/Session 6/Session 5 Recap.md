## Date
**In-Game Date:** Sunday, 6 Pharast, 4728 AR

## Summary

After restoring Eeler's skeletal body at the end of the previous session, Gib finally spoke with the inn's long-dead proprietor. Although Eeler's memories remain fragmented, enough returned to reveal that Eeler's Inn is far stranger than either of them first believed. During the conversation, Gib cured both himself and Hollis of the rat-borne disease while Eeler slowly pieced together portions of his past and the nature of the inn.

By the end of the night, Eeler's Inn reappeared in the material world outside the city of Jaha, giving Gib his first true glimpse of the city beyond the fog. Eeler entrusted Gib with a magical key allowing him to return to the inn, while Squibbles chose to accompany Gib as his permanent companion.

## Major Events

### Eeler Regains More of His Memory

Eeler remembered several important details:

- The inn was originally constructed along the **Olongo River**, not wherever it had previously appeared.
- He founded the inn after leaving a traveling circus and later serving as a court jester.
- His goal was to create a welcoming place for travelers after finally finding a place where he himself felt accepted.
- During his lifetime, the inn secretly operated as a speakeasy during one of Jaha's periods of prohibition.

Although his memories continue returning in pieces, he still struggles to distinguish what he remembers from what he merely senses through the inn.

### Gib and Hollis Recover

Using Medicine, Gib successfully treated both his own illness and Hollis's, removing the lingering effects of the diseased rats encountered throughout the inn.

Hollis finally met Eeler. Unable to hear Eeler's telepathic speech, he simply saw Gib apparently talking to an animated skeleton and remained understandably skeptical.

### The Nature of the Inn

Eeler revealed several disturbing discoveries.

He believes the inn itself has become his **phylactery**, though he has no memory of intentionally becoming a lich.

He also believes the inn no longer exists entirely within ordinary reality. Instead, it occupies some kind of space "between" places. This explained why people had become trapped inside and why time outside behaved strangely.

Despite existing outside normal reality, Gib and Hollis physically occupy the inn rather than merely projecting into it.

### Molak

Gib shared everything he knew about his own origins, including:

- emerging from a mirror,
- Molak destroying his village,
- his inability to see himself in ordinary mirrors,
- his strange connection to Molak,
- and his growing unease over unexplained dreams and memories.

Eeler found nearly all of it unbelievable.

However, one revelation stood out.

Eeler remembered a man named **Molak** from over two centuries ago.

According to Eeler:

- Molak was a studious astrologer.
- He likely came from Jaha.
- He once stayed at Eeler's Inn during Eeler's lifetime.
- Eeler had personally known him.

This immediately conflicted with everything Gib believed possible, as a normal dromaar could not have survived for over two hundred years.

### Dreams

Eeler explained that dreams are not merely imagination.

Dreams are tied to an actual plane of existence, and souls genuinely travel there while sleeping. Although Eeler knew little beyond common knowledge, he suggested Gib's experiences might be connected to that reality rather than simple nightmares.

## Eeler's Inn Returns

Curious about the strange fog outside, Eeler opened the front door.

Instead of whatever had surrounded the inn previously, the building slowly settled back into the world.

When the fog finally cleared, the inn stood:

- along the Olongo River,
- outside Jaha's main inhabited districts,
- isolated between the growing modern settlement and the ancient city.

No dramatic magical event accompanied its arrival. Aside from a single fisherman briefly noticing an unfamiliar riverside building, the inn simply became another structure on the riverbank.

## Gifts

### Key to Eeler's Inn

Recognizing that he could not easily leave the inn himself, Eeler entrusted Gib with the **Key to Eeler's Inn**.

The magical key allows Gib to return to the inn under extraordinary circumstances and guarantees that Eeler's hospitality will always remain open to him.

### Squibbles

Before leaving, Gib offered Squibbles the choice between remaining safely with Eeler or continuing the journey.

Squibbles chose Gib.

Mechanically, Squibbles became Gib's permanent pet companion.

## Departure

Before Gib departed, Eeler asked only one thing:

Bring people back.

The inn had become painfully lonely over the centuries, and Eeler wished to see it filled with guests once again.

Eeler also encouraged Gib to investigate Jaha, believing records there might explain the Molak he remembered from long ago.

## Closing Scene

As Gib stepped outside into the warm spring night, Jaha stretched across the river before him—partially restored, partially reclaimed by jungle, alive with workers, officials, and scholars rebuilding the ancient city.

Elsewhere throughout Jaha, several influential figures continued grappling with engineering failures, political decisions, archaeological discoveries, and mounting structural concerns, entirely unaware that an impossible riverside inn had quietly appeared just beyond the edge of the city.

The adventure now moves beyond the inn and into Jaha itself.
