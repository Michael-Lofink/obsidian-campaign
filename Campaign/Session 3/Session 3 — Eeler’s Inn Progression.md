**Perception DC 14**  
The candle flame is too steady for the draft moving through the old inn.

**Occultism DC 15**  
The light has a faint supernatural quality. It feels like the building itself is maintaining the flame.

**Esoteric Lore / Diverse Lore DC 15**  
Gib recognizes that the candle is functioning like a signal, a ward, and an invitation.

Let Gib attempt to walk away from the inn.

**Survival or Nature DC 15**

**Occultism DC 15**

**Perception DC 15**  
Gib hears the inn before he sees it again: a floorboard creak, a distant bell, or the same candle-lit window.

## Purpose

This scene establishes that the inn is haunted by more than rats. It also teaches that a haunt can be disabled or settled, allowing Hollis to move the barricade and expand the safe area.

## Possible Approaches

**Religion**  
Gib blesses the threshold, acknowledges the dead traveler, or calls for the spirit to rest.

**Occultism**  
Gib identifies the repeated spiritual pattern and interrupts it.

**Esoteric Lore / Diverse Lore**  
Allow if Gib describes an appropriate method involving names, thresholds, travel customs, or haunt behavior.

## After the Haunt

If Gib disables or settles the haunt:

> The footsteps stop.
> 
> For the first time since you entered the inn, the passage beyond the barricade sounds like empty wood again.
> 
> Hollis listens for several seconds.
> 
> Then he lowers his hammer.
> 
> “Right. That’s farther than I’ve gotten in days.”

Hollis can now move the barricade forward.

# Scene 4: First-Floor Objective

**Reference:** [[Session 3 — Eeler’s Inn Reference#Room Claiming|Room Claiming]], [[Session 3 — Eeler’s Inn Reference#POI Token Guide|POI Token Guide]]

The main first-floor goal is to reach the stairs safely enough to access the second floor.

Gib does not need to clear every room. He needs to expand the refuge through enough of the first floor to create a route to the stairs.

## Intended Route

Entrance refuge → bar / kitchen → main dining room → central common area → eastern dining section → southern stairs.

## Room Claiming

A room becomes claimed if Gib and Hollis can do most of the following:

- Light it
    
- Clear immediate threats
    
- Block major rat paths or doors
    
- Establish a physical connection to the current safe area
    
- Prevent the room’s haunt or spirit from immediately resetting
    

Claimed rooms are safe enough for Hollis to move through and reinforce.

# Scene 13: Seventh Witness Reveal — Reflective Glass

**Reference:** [[Session 3 — Eeler’s Inn Reference#Seventh Witness Reflective Glass Reveal|Seventh Witness Reflective Glass Reveal]]

Trigger this when a possessed rat or nest-connected creature is brought to 0 HP while Gib is invested in the Seventh Witness. This can happen earlier in the session. Keep this scene where it is as a reference point in the progression.

## Read Aloud

> The rat collapses, its body twitching once against the floorboards.
> 
> Its fur begins to harden.
> 
> The change starts at the eyes, spreading outward in thin silver cracks. Its body stiffens as flesh, bone, and blood become dark reflective glass.
> 
> For a moment, the surface catches the lantern light and shows something that is not in the room: a frightened face pressed against the inside of the rat’s reflection.
> 
> The image fades as the body finishes changing.
> 
> What remains is a small glass figure, shaped like the creature that died.

## What Gib Learns

Gib learns more about the Seventh Witness’s effect on creatures brought to 0 HP while he is invested in it.

The transformation does not simply destroy the body. It creates a reflective glass remnant that can briefly show the spiritual or emotional presence that was inside the creature.

Because the rats are possessed, the glass may show both the dying rat and the spirit riding inside it.

## Limits

This does not reveal a new active ability.

The mirror is not replaying the past generally. It is showing distorted final impressions through creatures affected by its existing glass transformation.

> On and around the bed lie Eeler’s remains: gnawed bones, torn clothing, and one skeletal hand still curled toward the edge of the mattress.

## Restoring Eeler

Gib can restore Eeler by doing most of the following:

- Returning the master key to Eeler’s remains
    
- Reassembling enough of his bones
    
- Relighting the suite lamp
    
- Speaking Eeler’s name
    
- Acknowledging him as proprietor
    
- Using the guest ledger information
    

## Possible Skill Checks

Use checks only if the moment needs uncertainty.

**Occultism DC 15**  
Understand how the key, remains, room, and inn are connected.

**Religion DC 15**  
Separate foreign spirits from Eeler’s soul.

**Medicine DC 14**  
Arrange the remains respectfully and correctly.

**Esoteric Lore / Diverse Lore DC 15**  
Recognize the restoration as a matter of identity, hospitality, and sympathetic connection.

A failed check should not completely block Eeler’s rescue if Gib has the key and understands the situation. Failure can cost time, cause a brief backlash, or require an additional step.
