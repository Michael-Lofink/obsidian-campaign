You are difficult for one or more creatures to see due to thick fog or some other obscuring feature. You can be [[Concealed|concealed]] to some creatures but not others. While [[Concealed|concealed]], you can still be [[observed]], but you're tougher to target. A creature that you're [[Concealed|concealed]] from must succeed at a DC 5 flat check when targeting you with an attack, spell, or other effect. If the check fails, you aren't affected. Area effects aren't subject to this flat check.
