# Informal Recap
[[Campaign/Session 6/Progression#Table of Contents|Back to Table Of Contents]]
<u>*The Hidden Library* by Joshua Kyan Aalampour</u>
Read aloud:
- Last session, Gib restored the body of Eeler, the long-dead proprietor of Eeler’s Inn. Eeler awakened as a skeleton with golden light burning in his empty eye sockets, while his scattered memories continued to return.
- Gib treated the disease carried by the inn’s rats, curing himself and beginning Hollis’s recovery. Hollis remained at the inn to rest.
- Eeler discovered that the inn itself had become his phylactery. Its rooms exist somewhere between physical places, allowing the building and its occupants to move in ways Eeler does not yet understand.
- Eeler remembered that his inn originally stood along the Olongo River outside Jaha. He also remembered Molak as a dromaar astrologer who lived in Jaha more than two centuries ago.
- Gib told Eeler about his birth from the mirror, Molak’s destruction of his village, and the strange experiences that have followed him. Eeler explained that dreams connect to a real plane of existence, though he could offer few answers about Gib’s connection to Molak.
- Eeler returned the inn to the material world along the Olongo River and gave Gib a magical key that could guide him back through certain forms of supernatural travel.
- As Gib prepared to leave, Squibbles chose to follow him and became his permanent companion.
- Gib stepped outside just after midnight on 6 Pharast. Across the river, the pale terraces of Jaha were waking beneath the full moon.

## In the Western-Annex
Read aloud:
>
>
>Warehouses and workshops crowd closer together ahead. Their walls have been built from timber, canvas, river stone, and pieces of pale masonry taken from the ancient city. Lanterns hang from ropes stretched between buildings, joined by shallow beds of blue-green fungi glowing beneath the eaves.
>
>The Western Annex is already deep into its working day. Dockhands unload sacks of grain and crates of excavation tools from low riverboats. Hammers sound from an open workshop where a damaged pulley is being repaired. Expedition crews gather around painted route boards, checking packs and counting coils of rope before leaving for the terraces. Vendors pour dark coffee from clay pots and serve bowls of spiced vegetables, river fish, and hot grain to workers beginning their shifts.
>
>Humans and iruxis move through the same crowded lanes alongside foreign scholars, merchants, guards, and laborers. Some wear clean clothes and carry sealed permits. Others are covered in clay, dust, or river mud. Conversations overlap in Mwangi, Iruxi, and Lirgeni, broken occasionally by the scrape of a cartwheel or a warning shouted from the docks.
>
>Above the roofs, Jaha’s old walls and terraces rise beneath Somal. Lamps mark the inhabited streets. Scaffolds and rope barriers mark the places still being reclaimed. Beyond them, sections of the Pale City remain dark beneath the jungle.
>
>A public notice board stands beside the road ahead, crowded with route closures, employment offers, missing-person notices, and government orders. Nearby, a young woman holds a folded river map beneath one arm. She studies the muddy path behind Gib, then looks past him toward Eeler’s Inn.

### Things that can be stumbled into:
- **Shora Mujafor:** She is investigating the change in Inali’s woven chart and the report of an unrecorded structure beside the river.
- **Public notice board:** Contains West Terrace notices, route closures, expedition work, Moon Fortress warnings, missing-person reports, and waystone restrictions.
- **Night food stalls:** Provide food, magical coffee, rumors, and basic information about the city.
- **Expedition outfitters:** Workers discuss Versenne’s foreign partnerships and the equipment dispute involving West Terrace.
- **Restoration workers:** An iruxi crew returning by boat discusses a site placed under Barashzi’s authority.
- **Port office:** Holds current river surveys, dock registrations, and basic property records.

### Things That Require Asking

- Where civic and property records are stored.
- Where old astrological records are kept.
- Who controls records concerning the pre-Jahan iruxi court.
- How visitors gain access to restricted records or excavation sites.
- Whether anyone recognizes Eeler’s name.
- Whether anyone recognizes Molak’s name.
- How to reach Jhebyiti, the Kehwamesi, or Star-Crossed Charts.

### Things that can only be discovered:
 - Inali’s woven chart shifted toward the river at approximately the same time Eeler’s Inn appeared.
- No current or discontinued map at Star-Crossed Charts shows the inn at this location.
- Molak’s apparent age places the investigation within a disputed historical period involving pre-Jahan iruxi history.
- Information about Molak may be divided among civic archives, ancestral testimony, astrological records, and excavation reports.
- Shora can arrange for Oakuma to meet Gib at Star-Crossed Charts.
- Shora can also ask Oakuma to visit Eeler’s Inn if Eeler cannot leave it.
- Oakuma will want Eeler to identify old locations directly on a map.
- Access to protected records will require cooperation from the institution that controls them.

### Possible Scene Endpoints
- Gib agrees to bring Eeler’s ledger to Star-Crossed Charts.
- Shora agrees to bring Oakuma to Eeler’s Inn.
- Gib decides which type of record he wants to pursue first.
- Gib receives directions into Jhebyiti and the public areas of the old city.
- Gib reaches the crossing into Jaha with a contact name and a clear next destination.
